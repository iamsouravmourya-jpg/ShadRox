# ShadRox beta terms

ShadRox is an experimental beta. Use it only with backups or source control and
review all generated edits and commands before relying on them.

The software is provided for testing and feedback, without a promise of
availability, correctness, security, or fitness for a particular purpose.
You are responsible for your files, provider accounts, API keys, and any
commands you approve. Do not use the beta for safety-critical or regulated
work.

You may stop using the beta at any time. Report defects and data-deletion
requests in the private beta WhatsApp group. ShadRox is free and runs on bring-your-own-key:
there is no plan to buy and no charge from ShadRox — your own provider key carries every request
and your provider bills you directly.
